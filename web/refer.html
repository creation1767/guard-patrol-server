Referrals

Refer a security company. You both get a month free.

Know another guard company that's still on paper logs? Send them your link. When they pay their first month after the free trial, we credit one free month to your company and one to theirs.

How it works

  1. Share your referral link: 4horsemansecurity.com/signup?ref=YOURCODE. Replace YOURCODE with your company code, the one your officers use to sign in.
  2. Or the other company types your company code in the "Referred by" box when they sign up.
  3. When their first monthly payment goes through, we apply a credit equal to one month of each company's plan to your next invoices.

The fine print

  • One free month for each company per referred company that becomes a paying customer.
  • The credit is applied to future invoices. It has no cash value and can't be transferred.
  • Your company must have an active subscription when the credit is applied.
