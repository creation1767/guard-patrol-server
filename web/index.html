What's included

Everything a guard shift needs, in one place

GPS check-in & check-out

Officers check in at their assigned site. The geofence flags anyone outside the perimeter, and a live timer runs for the whole shift.

QR guard tours

Print QR labels for up to 20 tour locations per site. Officers scan each one, log all-clear or an issue, and you see every round.

Reports straight to you

Incident, daily activity and maintenance reports with photos and GPS go to the administrator's inbox. Mark them reviewed and the officer sees it.

Post orders

Write post orders per site. Officers acknowledge them at check-in, and any change makes everyone acknowledge again.

Team messaging

A dispatch channel for the whole company and a channel for each site, with quick replies like "10-4" and "Need backup".

Location only on shift

Officers are located only while checked in. Monitoring stops the moment they check out, and off-duty staff never appear.

Questions

Good to know

Who is the administrator?

The person who subscribes. The administrator adds and removes officers, sets up sites, post orders and tour locations, and receives every report.

How do officers sign in?

With your company code, their badge number and the PIN you give them. Your company code appears after you subscribe and on your account page.

Is our data kept separate from other companies?

Yes. Each company has its own officers, sites, reports and messages, and no company can see another's.

What happens if we cancel?

You keep full access until the end of the month you've paid for. When the subscription ends, your company account and all of its data are permanently deleted.

What if a monthly payment fails?

Your subscription renews automatically every 30 days. If a payment fails, you have 15 days from the payment date to pay or update your card from your account page. If it is still unpaid after 15 days, the company account and all of its data are permanently deleted.

Can my clients see our work?

Yes. Give each client a portal login and they can see the reports, check-ins and guard-tour scans for their own sites, view their invoices and send you service requests.

Can we change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime from the Overview tab of your dashboard. The price difference is worked out to the day on your next invoice. To downgrade, your sites and officers must fit the smaller plan.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. Officers on iPhone can use the full app in a browser by logging in on this website.
